Omitted Promoter Variables & Risk Register

Key factors omitted from simplified baseline
High Impact F1 Sanctioning / Hosting Rights Fee

Formula One Group charges host promoters $25M–$50M USD (₹200 Cr–₹400 Cr) per year, typically funded through state subsidies or private underwriting. Omitted from base operating cost.

Medium Impact Circuit Homologation & Track Upgrades

Buddh International Circuit recertification, FIA Grade 1 compliance, modern electronic marshaling, kerb safety adjustments, and tech upgrades.

Regulatory Tax Classification & Legal Disputes

Historically, the 2011–2013 races were classified as entertainment rather than sport by Uttar Pradesh tax authorities, triggering severe entertainment and customs levies.

Economic Regional Leakage & Displacement

Attendee spending cannot be equated directly with net economic benefit due to regional substitution effects and leakage to national hospitality and travel chains.
